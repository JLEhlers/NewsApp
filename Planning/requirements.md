Functional requirements

* Readers can view a list of approved news articles.
* Readers can open an article to read it.
* Articles display their title, author, created\_at date, and description.
* Independent journalists can create articles.
* Editors can approve or reject submitted articles.
* Only approved articles appear in the public news feed.
* Users can log in and access features permitted by their role.



Non-functional requirements

* Usability: The website should be easy to navigate.
* Security: Users must only access features they are authorized to use.
* Performance: Articles should load within a reasonable time.
* Maintainability: The project should use clear, organized code.
